# 〔成都鈴醫書〕（1881 年抄記，柏林 Slg. Unschuld 8555） / Cheng du ling yi shu [An itinerant physician’s text from Chengdu]: Transcription Dataset

《〔成都鈴醫書〕》是柏林國家圖書館藏 Slg. Unschuld 8555 的晚清鈴醫手稿，末署光緒七年（1881）抄記。書中有遊方醫者對鄉親的說詞、求藥問答、韻語，以及眼科等方條，可研究行醫者如何取得信任、描述病候及傳授知識。

Cheng du ling yi shu, Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin Slg. Unschuld 8555, is a late Qing manuscript with an 1881 copying note. It records an itinerant physician's speech to local audiences, exchanges with people seeking treatment, verses, and entries on subjects such as eye complaints.

39 幅皆有定位，收入 176 正文欄、末葉抄記及小字。第 39 幅是首正文葉加色卡的重拍，連回第 5 幅。

All 39 scans are indexed, with 176 main-text columns and colophon writing. Scan 39 is a color-target exposure of the page transcribed from scan 5.
